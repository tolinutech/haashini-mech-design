01 — ABOUT US

Practical engineering for unique manufacturing challenges.

Haashini Mech Design was founded in January 2024 to provide innovative solutions in mechanical conceptual design. Initially focused on machinery design, the company evolved to offer complete special purpose machines, fixtures and material handling equipment.

This integrated approach helps provide seamless, efficient and tailored solutions for specific manufacturing and material handling challenges.
